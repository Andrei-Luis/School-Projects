Unlock your potential with computer programming

Are you looking for an elective that gives you actual, real-world skills for the future? As current Senior High
students here at PCU, we highly recommend choosing the Computer Programming subject!

You don't need any prior coding or tech experience to get started. In this class, you will learn how to think logically,
solve problems step-by-step, and build your own awesome digital projects. Watch our short promo video below to see what our
student experience is all about!

What You Will Learn

Practical Coding: Learn to build actual web apps and clean digital projects from scratch.
Problem Solving: Train your brain to think logically and handle complex problems step-by-step.
Tech Foundations: Gain essential skills for the future, even if you have zero background today.
